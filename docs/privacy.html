Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

“Evermore Romance Bot” (the “App”) is a private automation tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Evermore Romance Piano (youtube.com/@EvermoreRomancePiano) to manage that channel.

What the App accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), the App uses the YouTube API Services to upload the channel's own videos, set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and publish times, add them to the channel's playlists, and read view statistics of the channel's own videos.

What the App does not do

  • It does not collect, store or share any personal data of viewers or other users.
  • It does not sell or transfer any data to third parties and does not use data for advertising.
  • It does not access any account other than the channel owner's own account.

Storage and security

The OAuth refresh token and video statistics are stored only on the channel owner's private server and are used only to operate the channel. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, after which the stored token stops working and is deleted.

YouTube and Google

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the channel: youtube.com/@EvermoreRomancePiano.
