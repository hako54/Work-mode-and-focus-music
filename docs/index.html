Evermore Romance Piano

Evermore Romance Piano is a YouTube channel of calm, romantic and relaxing instrumental music (youtube.com/@EvermoreRomancePiano).

This site describes the small private tool (“Evermore Romance Bot”) that the channel owner uses to upload the channel's own videos to YouTube through the YouTube Data API. The tool is used only by the channel owner; it is not offered to other users.
